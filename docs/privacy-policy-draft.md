# Privacy Policy Draft

PlayGrid Club collects and processes only the data needed to coordinate sports participation for an organization.

## Data collected

- Name
- Email address
- Department
- Avatar URL
- Sports preferences and skill level
- Booking, game, group, and notification activity

## How data is used

- Authenticate users
- Show relevant sports and venues
- Create and manage bookings
- Coordinate games, groups, and events
- Deliver account and booking notifications

## Data sharing

- Data is not sold
- Data may be stored in Supabase for application operation
- Admins may see venue and booking information needed to coordinate sports activity

## Retention

- User data remains until account deletion is processed or the organization requests removal
